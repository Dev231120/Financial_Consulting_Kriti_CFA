Project Title: Dual-Sector Equity Research and Financial Consulting Framework: Legacy IT vs New Age Tech Platforms
Tournament: KRITI Intra-IIT Guwahati Inter-Hostel Championship (Low Prep Financial Consulting Event)
Team Assignment: Strategic Analyst and Financial Modeler

Project Overview
This project was developed for the Financial Consulting challenge of the KRITI Inter-Hostel championship at IIT Guwahati. The objective of the study was to diagnose, analyze, and value two contrasting pillars of the Indian technology ecosystem: the stable, mature Legacy IT Services sector (including TCS, Infosys, Wipro, and HCL Tech) and the hyper-growth, disruptive New Age Tech-driven platform economy (including Zomato/Eternal, Swiggy, Nykaa, and Meesho). By analyzing operational metrics, accounting structures, regulatory changes, and digital trends, the team created an integrated financial valuation engine and structural sector report.

Key Deliverables Delivered

A comprehensive 12-slide Investment Deck detailing qualitative sectoral frameworks, competitive advantages, behavioral profiles, and risk mitigations.

A fully dynamic Excel Financial Workbook modeling historical and 5-year forward macro trajectories, capital structure layers, and valuation sensitivities.

Core Analytical Frameworks Covered

Sector Diagnosis and Structural Trajectories
Evaluated the financial parameters of both sectors over a trailing 5-year baseline and a 5-year forward projection horizon. Legact IT was diagnosed through productivity metrics like Operating Profit (EBIT) margins, utilization rates, and employee cost-to-revenue ratios amid an AI-first evolution. New Age Tech platforms were analyzed through transactional metrics such as Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) growth, Average Order Value (AOV), Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) to Lifetime Value (LTV) ratios, and path-to-profitability burn multiples.

Conceptual Financial Acumen and Statement Selection
The project highlights specific financial statement selection priorities depending on the industry asset type. The Income Statement (P and L) was isolated as the primary Productivity Ledger for mature IT services to evaluate operational efficiency, labor cost decoupling, and operating leverage. The Cash Flow Statement was established as the primary Survival Predictor for New Age Tech platforms to track true Free Cash Flow trajectories, working capital efficiency, and runway preservation over cash-burn metrics.

Advanced Valuation Mechanics (10-Year DCF and Sensitivity Engineering)
Constructed custom 10-year DCF models instead of standard 5-year horizons to capture structural industry cycles.

TCS Valuation Model: Modeled a mature, cash-generating business capturing stable revenue trajectories, high-end return metrics (ROIC), and a steady AI transition cycle using a base WACC of 11.15% and a terminal growth rate of 4%.

Eternal (Zomato) Valuation Model: Mapped an operational S-curve capturing high-growth phases (35% initial growth scaling to 7% terminal expansion) alongside margin expansion from a baseline of 1.85% up to a stable 17% threshold, utilizing a base WACC of 11.26% and a terminal growth rate of 4%.

Matrix Sensitivities: Developed dynamic sensitivity models calculating intrinsic share value fluctuations across shifts in Cost of Capital (WACC plus or minus 2%) and Terminal Growth Rates (plus or minus 1%).

Behavioral Finance and Investment Advisory
Engineered age-stratified investment playbooks distinguishing client profiles. Advised low-risk, dividend-yielding defensive allocations for parents using the stable free cash flow, lower beta, and high capital protection profile of TCS. Recommended aggressive wealth compounding for younger personal portfolios via the high-growth trajectory and digital ecosystem dominance of Eternal.

M and A Strategy (Corporate Joint-Forces Report)
Authored an institutional-grade merger and acquisition defense paper analyzing structural consolidations. Evaluated the financial and technological synergies of a hypothetical acquisition of Eternal by the Tata ecosystem (via Tata Digital). The report details quantitative logistics optimization via automated routing solutions, procurement savings within the B2B supply segment, and platform cross-selling opportunities across domestic customer networks.

Competition Context
This research was evaluated under standard corporate consulting frameworks. Scoring was heavily weighted toward Technical Valuation Mechanics (30%), Conceptual Financial Acumen (20%), Financial Diagnosis (15%), Investment Rationale (15%), Behavioral Finance Advisory (15%), and Report and Model Hygiene (10%).
